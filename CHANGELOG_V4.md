# V4 visual refinement notes

This pass responds directly to the latest screenshot review.

## Typography
- Removed the narrow/compressed display stack that was making `LOCAL + GLOBAL`, `KARACHI`, `08` and `100+` feel stretched.
- Replaced it with Inter Tight + a Helvetica/Arial fallback and softened extreme negative tracking.
- Rebalanced hero, card, project and contact display sizing to keep the site editorial rather than poster-like.

## Live metrics
- `08` and `100+` now count up only when their section enters the viewport.
- The same behavior is reused in the Production Network section.
- Animation runs once per visit and respects reduced-motion settings through the site's existing motion controls.

## Way We Work / Our Strength
- Headline blocks were lifted higher inside the cards.
- Copy width is constrained for faster reading.
- Mobile card height/spacing was tightened as well.

## Production Network
- Replaced the pale market board with a cinematic black/red treatment inspired by the stronger earlier direction.
- Left side: animated red `08` production-market metric.
- Right side: staggered market rows with restrained hover motion and red activation line.
- Bottom: animated `100+` production metric with supporting copy.

## Scope unchanged
- Selected Work remains a strict 3-column desktop grid.
- Services and Upcoming Ventures remain hidden until client content is finalized.
- Provisional logo treatment remains intentionally replaceable.
